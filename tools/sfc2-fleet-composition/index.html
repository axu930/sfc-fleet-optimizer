Fleet Composition Analyzer

Find the smallest fleet in your chosen ratios that is estimated to clear a target within 3, 4, 5, or 6 rounds. Compare expected losses, rebuild costs, and the transports needed to bring home the plunder.

How to use: Enter integer ratio weights for your attack fleet, the defender's ships and defenses, both sides' combat tech, and the target's resources. Calculate to compare four round limits. Each cargo estimate keeps that attack ratio fleet fixed and adds one transport type at a time.

Model notes

Each shot selects a random target from the full opposing roster. The model estimates rapid-fire chains from the current target mix, resolves simultaneous round fire, resets shields, carries hull damage forward, and estimates hull explosions. The clear-confidence estimate treats survival as independent per ship within each class, using each class's expected remaining fraction; actual battles can vary.

Transport counts are added after the round-limit fleet is selected. Each transport option is simulated with that fixed attack fleet, so the cargo estimate includes expected losses without changing the chosen combat ratio. Build and rebuild costs are shown by resource; travel fuel is not included.
