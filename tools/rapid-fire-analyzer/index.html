Model notes

Rapid-fire ratings are based on the SFC2 Wiki Rapid Fire reference. For rating R, the chance to continue after hitting that target is (R − 1) / R. The expected shot count is the geometric chain using the defender ratio as a fixed target probability.

Unlisted rapid-fire matchups are treated as rating ×1. Weapon damage, target hull, and target shield use the shared unit data and the selected tech levels. A shot at or below 1% of the target’s shield is marked ineffective under the existing combat rule.
