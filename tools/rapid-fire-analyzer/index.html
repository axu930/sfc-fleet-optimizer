Model notes

Rapid-fire ratings are based on the SFC2 Wiki Rapid Fire reference. For rating R, the chance to continue after hitting that target is (R − 1) / R. The expected shot count is the geometric chain using your composition as a fixed target probability.

Unlisted rapid-fire matchups are treated as rating ×1. Rapid-fire ratings remain fixed as research changes; this estimate does not model target depletion or combat rounds.
