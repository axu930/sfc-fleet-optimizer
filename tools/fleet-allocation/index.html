Fleet Allocation Optimizer

Allocate a limited Zeus fleet across distinct NPC targets. Each target can be attacked at most once.

Add one location and espionage report per target, enter your available Zeus and acceptable loss limit, then calculate. DSP rewards partial destruction; raid resources require a full attacker win. Results are deterministic estimates, not a guarantee of battle outcome.

Allocation constraints

The loss limit applies to expected Zeus losses across all selected targets. Blank loss limit defaults to 0.1% of your available Zeus.

How to read these estimates

DSP and debris use the existing deterministic, six-round combat model. Because it retains fractional expected survivors instead of discrete ship counts, full-win probability is approximated as exp(−expected remaining non-Hephaestus defenders) × the estimated chance that at least one Zeus survives. For raid objectives, expected plunder is this estimated win probability times the conditional raid amount; DSP and gross debris remain expected combat outputs. This is not an exact in-game battle-winner calculation.

As elsewhere in this browser-only model, arithmetic uses JavaScript numbers, so individual counts above 253 are approximate. The parsed preview preserves the pasted count text to make it easier to verify what the report said.

On a successful attack against a report with no defenses, the tool applies the supplied rule of 7/8 of reported resources across the first wave and two effective follow-up waves (1/2, 1/4, and 1/8). Otherwise, it counts at most one 1/2-resource raid. Carmanor totals are calculated per wave at 125,000 cargo each, without making cargo capacity a solver constraint.
