Model notes

This is an expected-value approximation for very large fleets. Combat is simultaneous within each round and capped at six rounds; shields reset while hull damage persists. The expected curve uses mean Zeus rapid fire, while the conservative curve reduces expected RF shots by 4% using the existing 2σ stress case.

DSP is the modeled ship build value destroyed divided by 1,000. Debris is 30% of the destroyed ships’ ore and crystal build costs; hydrogen and defenses contribute no debris. The UI reports ore and crystal separately, while Dionysus counts divide their combined total by the 20,000 cargo capacity of one recycler. Net points is DSP plus generated ore/crystal debris divided by 1,000, minus the full lost Zeus build cost divided by 1,000, including hydrogen. Threat removed remains a diagnostic based on defender fire that passes the Zeus 1% shield rule.
