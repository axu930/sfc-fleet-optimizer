Model notes

This is an expected-value approximation tailored to very large fleets. Defender fire is distributed over Zeus with a hull-bucket / hit-distribution model; Zeus rapid fire uses the expected geometric-chain length. Combat is simultaneous and capped at six rounds. DSP counts destroyed ships only; defenses still absorb Zeus shots and fire back but do not add DSP.

Threat removed is a diagnostic, not an in-game score: it is the fraction of initial defender weapon output remaining after excluding shots that cannot pass the Zeus 1% shield threshold. It helps distinguish “DSP removed” from “dangerous units removed.”

The efficiency knee is heuristic. It finds the largest normalized bend in DSP destruction versus log Zeus count among points satisfying the selected survival threshold. Treat it as a useful candidate, not a uniquely optimal answer.
